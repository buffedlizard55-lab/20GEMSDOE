## 2. Dissertation-Committee Promotion Gate Rules

1. **Spatial Dev / Vault Partition**: Each of the 4 geographic quadrants is partitioned into 4 spatial Development sub-blocks (`16` Dev sub-blocks total, `80.0%` of footprint = `4,133,899` pixels) and `1` interior Vault Holdout strip (`4` strips total, `20.0%` of footprint = `1,033,474` pixels).
2. **Multiple-Comparisons Control**: All pre-registered hypotheses are tested across the 16 Dev sub-blocks with explicit **Holm-Bonferroni step-down FWER** ($\alpha = 0.05$) and **Benjamini-Hochberg step-up FDR** ($q = 0.05$) adjustments.
3. **Single-Shot Vault Holdout Gate**: Only candidates that pass the 4-quadrant holdout gate may touch the 20% Vault Holdout, and each candidate is evaluated on the Vault Holdout **at most once** (`VaultHoldoutGate`).
