## How this changes the statistical model

Incomplete mapping motivates positive–unlabeled learning, not treating zeros as verified negatives. Mapping selection depends plausibly on exposure, scale, age and survey effort; **SCAR and correct positives are not established**. A power-law tail alone cannot identify hidden pixel prevalence: trace splitting, footprint clipping, cutoff, overlap and chosen corridor width change the class definition. The actual primary tail scenario was rejected (399-refit KS bootstrap p=.0025), so it cannot be advertised as an identified calibration prior.

The official continuous DTI rewards maximum kernel-weighted credit per truth pixel and penalizes weighted prediction mass. It is not a proper probability-calibration scoring rule. Utility-oriented confidence fields and empirical fault probabilities are different objects. Genuine reliability/Brier needs independent hidden positives **and** representative adjudicated background with documented ascertainment, not catalogue thinning or synthetic targets.

The local hash-pinned competition/GeoDAWN/DEM stack and inherited GDR footprint extracts are obtainable now. Bridge integrity does not prove official-host byte authenticity. Landsat and 3DHP are free official products, but actual footprint scenes/QA and hydrography coverage have not been acquired: both remain **blocked**, not viable promises. New MT, groundwater or subsurface inversion products are not asserted available.

Do not reinterpret failed H22-2/H22-3 controls as a license to tune. No new model or weekly upload is recommended. Priority is independent evidence and an adequately powered preregistered design; the reused four quadrants and old Vault cannot become an untouched final set.
