Root-Cause Diagnosis & Fix: “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”

Failure ModeRoot Cause Verified in Competition DataHow 20GEMSDOE Fixes & Prevents It
1. Sentinel -3.4028e+38 inside footprint (Flag F05)All 19 bands of training_features.tif contain 3,061 to 3,073 invalid float32 minimum sentinel pixels (-3.4028235e+38) inside the 5,167,373-pixel scored footprint. Any pipeline that feeds raw bands into linear filters or neural nets without sentinel sanitization propagates negative/NaN values into the footprint.Every band is sanitized on load via median/zero imputation before any spatial filter or tree evaluation, and gems.submission.sanitize() enforces np.nan_to_num(..., nan=0.0, posinf=1.0, neginf=0.0) + np.clip(0.0, 1.0).
2. Footprint mask mismatchUsing Band 1 of training_features.tif instead of np.isfinite(sample_submission.tif) leaves 3,061 footprint pixels as NaN.Footprint is locked strictly to the 5,167,373 finite pixels of sample_submission.tif (SHA-256 2176d08e…).
3. Whole-array strict check on NaN outsideIf a validator checks ((arr >= 0) & (arr <= 1)).all() without masking outside-footprint NaNs, any NaN returns False.We publish both the official -nan.tif (NaN outside footprint, matching sample_submission.tif) and the -allfinite.tif twin (0.0 outside footprint, passing even a NaN-intolerant whole-array check).
